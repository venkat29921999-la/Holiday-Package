Our story

It began with one bad holiday

In 2012 our founder booked a package that promised paradise and delivered a parking lot view. So she started planning trips the way she wished someone had planned hers: with real local people, honest prices and zero surprises that were not the good kind. Today that idea carries more than forty thousand travellers to eighty five destinations.

Every itinerary is still hand-built by a human travel designer who has actually been there, because the best holidays are never copied and pasted.

Our journey

Fourteen years, one flight path

Follow the plane down the line and watch each milestone open like a camera lens.

2012

The first trip

Three friends, one rented van and a handwritten itinerary. Twelve guests joined us, and eleven came back the following year.

2015

Going international

Our first overseas packages launched with flights, stays and guides under a single roof.

2018

Hand-picked resorts

We opened our signature stays collection, every property personally visited and rated by our team.

2021

Cruises & escapes

Ocean voyages and flexible bookings arrived, so travelling again felt calm and completely safe.

2026

40,000 stories and counting

Eighty five destinations, a team of one hundred twenty and a promise that has not changed since day one.
